  Application developed to simplify the creation and configuration of bots through visual canvas flows.

excerpt: Application for creating and configuring bots through visual canvas flows, with interface redesign and improved navigation experience.
objective: Offer a more **intuitive and visual way to create and configure bots**, combining a canvas to represent flows with forms to configure the elements. The approach sought to make it easier to understand the bot's structure and behavior compared to the predominantly form-based configuration used in the main application.
what_i_built: |-
  I predominantly worked on **readjusting the layout and evolving the application's user experience**, prototyping the interfaces to align them with the **Design System I was also responsible for evolving and maintaining**, already used in other company projects.

  During this process, I took the opportunity to apply a series of **UI and UX improvements**, reviewing the organization of information, menu options, modules, tools, and other elements of the application, making the product more **intuitive, practical, and consistent**.

  In addition to readjusting the existing screens, I was also responsible for **prototyping new screens**, defining their interfaces and interaction experiences according to the needs that arose during the product's evolution.

  On some occasions, I also participated directly in **implementing the code changes**, although that was not the main focus of my work on the project.
challenge: |-
  One of the main challenges was **readjusting the interface to the established Design System**, ensuring that the components, visual patterns, and behaviors used in Bot Studio were aligned with the company's other projects.

  I also carried out an analysis of **well-established products on the market with similar proposals and structures**, seeking to identify interaction, organization, and functionality patterns that could be relevant for Bot Studio. Based on these references, I evaluated which approaches could be adapted to the product's context, avoiding merely reproducing solutions from other applications.

  Beyond analyzing external references, it was also necessary to **study the application itself and understand its existing features and flows** to identify opportunities for improvement. Based on this analysis, I proposed several structural and functional changes, seeking to make the experience more intuitive and practical without compromising existing features.
result: |-
  The work resulted in a product **visually standardized with the company's other applications**, maintaining consistency with projects in which I had also worked on development and maintenance.

  The structural and experience changes provided a **more organized, practical, and intuitive application**, making it easier to navigate and use the different features and configuration options.

  Overall, the evolution of the interface and flows provided a **significant improvement in the user experience**, making interaction with the product clearer and more efficient.
