description: |-
  Institutional platforms developed with a focus on performance, technical SEO, accessibility, marketing tool integrations, and multilingual support (PT-BR and EN).

  I worked on the development and maintenance of Escallo's institutional website, implementing custom features and integrations with external services such as RD Station. I developed lead conversion flows with security validation using Cloudflare Turnstile before sending data to the RD Station API, in addition to custom Elementor integrations for dynamic mapping of custom fields registered in RD Station via Dynamic Tags. I also worked on implementing event tracking (gtag), performance optimizations, responsiveness, Core Web Vitals, and improvements aimed at the user's navigation experience.
excerpt: Institutional website with performance, technical SEO, and marketing integrations, featuring lead validation and event tracking.
objective: Strengthen the company's digital presence through an institutional website focused on performance, accessibility, SEO, and lead generation, offering a consistent experience across different devices and languages and enabling integration with the tools used by the marketing and sales teams.
what_i_built: |-
  I worked on the maintenance and evolution of the institutional website, on both the previous and the current version. In both, I took part in layout updates, interface improvements, responsiveness, and the implementation of custom features.

  I was also responsible for integrating the site's forms with the RD Station API, enabling the data submitted by users to be sent directly to the platform. To do this, I implemented submission flow management within the site itself, including data validation before sending.

  Additionally, I implemented form security validation using Cloudflare Turnstile and worked on implementing event tracking with gtag.

  I also worked on improvements related to performance, accessibility, responsiveness, and navigation experience, along with maintaining and updating the PT-BR and EN versions of the site.
challenge: |-
  One of the main challenges was the integration of the forms with RD Station. The platform provided a plugin/script to monitor the site's forms and identify events such as submission, but this mechanism did not work correctly when the forms were used inside modals.

  As an alternative, I chose to take control of the submission flow directly within the application. The solution started managing form submission manually, performing the required validations and, after confirming the data, sending the information directly to the RD Station API.

  I also faced challenges related to performance and Core Web Vitals, working on identifying the points that impacted page loading and the interaction experience. It was necessary to analyze the application's behavior and make adjustments to improve the site's performance and responsiveness, considering the different devices and access conditions.

  Another challenge was related to form security, integrating Cloudflare Turnstile into the submission flow to validate the user's interaction before sending the data.

  In addition, the evolution of the different versions of the site required reconciling layout, responsiveness, accessibility, and performance changes, maintaining a consistent experience while new features were incorporated.
result: |-
  The work contributed to the continuous evolution of the institutional website, maintaining a responsive, accessible, and consistent experience across different devices and languages.

  The direct integration with the RD Station API provided greater control over the form submission process, allowing conversion flows to work independently of the monitoring mechanism provided by the platform.

  Interface and performance improvements also contributed to a more consistent navigation experience across the different versions of the site.
