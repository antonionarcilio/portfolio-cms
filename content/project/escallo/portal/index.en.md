description: |-
  Application developed to **centralize access to different solutions in the company's ecosystem**, allowing the user to authenticate once and access products and features according to their permissions. **I worked as a frontend developer** throughout the development, maintenance, and continuous evolution of the product, also contributing to **UI/UX** in defining and improving some flows and interfaces.


  In partnership with another developer, I participated in **implementing the application from its initial conception**, developing core features such as the **access control system**, which allowed managing users and defining their respective **access levels**. Based on these levels, the available features for each user were determined, such as access to external solutions, **subscription and invoice management**, and a **sales pipeline MVP**.

  As the product evolved, I also took over the continuity of the **Design System**, whose initial structure had been prototyped by a designer. As the application matured, I began evolving components, visual patterns, and interface behaviors according to the needs identified during development.

  During this process, I also worked on **investigating and resolving technical and performance issues**. Among them, I diagnosed a behavior present only in production builds that caused shared layout components, such as the sidebar, to be remounted during navigation, causing unwanted re-execution of entry animations. After investigating the behavior of **Next.js's App Router**, I implemented manual control of the animations' state to ensure they ran only at the appropriate times.

  I also worked on **optimizing communication with the API**, reducing redundant requests by consolidating GraphQL queries into a single call per page.

challenge: |-
  Keeping the components consistent across different projects that shared the same Design System became a challenge as the applications evolved. To solve this, I developed a **shared component library**, later used in two other distinct products.

  I also faced challenges related to **navigation experience and responsiveness**, including redesigning part of the layout and defining a new approach for the sidebar, based on references from third-party products with hierarchical navigation at different levels.

  On the technical side, I investigated a behavior identified only in production that caused shared components to be remounted during navigation and animations to run improperly. The solution involved **manually managing the animations' state**, ensuring greater control over when they should run.

  Additionally, I worked on **optimizing communication with the API**, identifying redundant requests and consolidating GraphQL queries into more comprehensive calls during page loading.
