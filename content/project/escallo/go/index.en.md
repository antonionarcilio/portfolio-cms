description: |-
  Application built from scratch with the purpose of offering customers a leaner, more intuitive alternative to the main platform, reducing the complexity caused by the large number of settings, customizations, and available flows. I worked as a **Frontend Developer from the product's conception**, being responsible for the full implementation of the application and, later, for its maintenance and continuous evolution.

  Beyond development, I participated in **reworking the user experience**, redesigning flows from the main platform that involved many steps, customization options, and extensive, poorly structured forms. The goal was to turn these flows into more direct and intuitive journeys, reducing steps, distractions, and unnecessary decisions for the user.

  As the product evolved, I also took over the continuity of the **Design System**, whose initial structure had been prototyped by a designer. Building on that work, I began evolving components, visual patterns, and interface behaviors according to the needs identified during development.

  I also worked on **optimizing communication with the API**, reducing redundant requests by consolidating GraphQL queries into a single call per page.

challenge: |-
  One of the main challenges was **simplifying flows** originally designed for a more complete platform, **preserving essential features** while reducing the number of steps, settings, and decisions required from the user.

  I also faced challenges related to navigation experience and responsiveness, including reworking part of the layout and defining a **new approach for the sidebar**, seeking to organize different levels of information **without compromising the simplicity** proposed for the product.

  Since the application was developed simultaneously with other projects sharing the same Design System, it was also necessary to handle the **consistency and evolution of components** across different products. This context contributed to creating a **shared component library**.

  On the technical side, I investigated problems related to **animations running incorrectly** during navigation, caused by the remounting of shared components in production. The solution involved **manually managing animation state** to control when they should run.
result: |-
  The product resulted in a **modern, responsive, and visually consistent application**, with a **fluid navigation experience** and a *more direct approach* to flows that previously presented greater complexity.

  The **simplification of flows** and the organization of the interfaces contributed to a *more objective experience*, while the use of interactions and animations reinforced **visual feedback** and made the application more attractive and intuitive to use.
